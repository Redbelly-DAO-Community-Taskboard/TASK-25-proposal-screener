Proposal Summary (1–2 sentences)
This proposal activates non-locking staking (6% APY) for all unallocated RBNT in the DAO Treasury, turning idle tokens into a risk-free, instant-withdraw yield source. No budget requested, fully reversible, auditable monthly.

Problem & Rationale
A significant portion of the DAO’s RBNT Treasury is currently idle.
These tokens could generate stable, predictable yield without impacting liquidity.
The staking module is no-lock, meaning the DAO can unstake instantly whenever funds are needed.
Leaving RBNT inactive represents a loss of potential revenue, slows the DAO’s autonomy, and weakens long-term reserves.
This proposal solves the problem by making staking the default state of RBNT until needed.
