**Proposal Summary**  
This proposal executes the 50-day Communication Strategy defined in the official PDF, delivering six structured content formats (#1–#6) across defined channels. The initiative strengthens Redbelly’s institutional positioning and community engagement while remaining under 10% of the annual Community and Marketing budgets. (Canva link end of the proposal)

**Problem & Rationale** (list the problem you are trying to solve)  
Redbelly’s value proposition as a compliant, institutional-grade blockchain is often misunderstood due to common crypto-related misconceptions. Existing communication formats can lack engagement or clarity, making it harder to educate audiences, activate the community, and reinforce institutional credibility.  
This proposal funds the structured execution of the Communication Strategy PDF, ensuring consistent delivery, controlled budgeting, and measurable engagement outcomes over a 50-day release schedule.
