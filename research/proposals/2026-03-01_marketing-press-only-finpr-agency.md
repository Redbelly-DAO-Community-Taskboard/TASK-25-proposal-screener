DAO PROPOSAL — FINPR PRESS
RELEASE (EDITORIAL ONLY)

Relevant guilds :

Marketing / Community

**1. Proposal Summary**

This proposal requests DAO approval to fund a single editorial press release package with FINPR
for 2,890 USDT, complemented by a limited RBNT allocation (50,000 RBNT) to support
research, coordination, and delivery oversight.
The scope is strictly limited to press content only, with no influencers, no promotions, and no
operational dependencies.

**2. Problem & Rationale**

Problem
Redbelly currently lacks durable, third-party editorial references that can be reused across:
• ecosystem communication,
• community onboarding,
• partner discussions,
• DAO documentation.
Social content is ephemeral. Internal posts lack external validation.
The DAO needs neutral, professional media coverage that exists independently of events,
campaigns, or individuals.
Rationale
A press release published in recognized crypto media:
• creates persistent external credibility,
• improves discoverability and SEO,
• can be reused by the DAO and community without dependency on execution by Redbelly.

**3. Objectives & Strategic Fit**

Objectives
• Produce and publish one professional editorial press release
• Secure placement in recognized crypto media
• Create a reusable DAO-owned communication asset
Strategic Alignment
• Marketing guild : visibility and credibility
• Community guild : onboarding and education support

**4. Scope of Work & Deliverables**

Vendor Scope (FINPR)
• Press release drafting
• Editorial placement
• Publication links + final report
Contributor Scope (Rainbowmagician)
• Research and framing of narrative angles
• Evaluation and selection of press options
• Coordination with FINPR
• Quality control and final validation
• Delivery of final report to DAO

**5. Budget & Payment Structure**

Requested Budget
• USDT: 2,890 (vendor payment — FINPR)
• RBNT: 50,000 (Rainbowmagician — coordination & research)
Payment Terms
• USDT:70% upfront in usdt , 30% after FINPR completes all deliverables and obligations
• RBNT: released upon delivery of:
◦ final publication links
◦ coordination report
No upfront payments. No renewal implied.

**6. Feasibility & Timeline**

• Estimated execution: 2–3 weeks
• Single-cycle delivery
• No dependency on Redbelly events or schedules
• No duplication of existing DAO efforts

**7. Oversight & Accountability**

• Oversight: Any pod leader or HC
• Validation criteria:
◦ confirmed publication
◦ links delivered
◦ coordination report submitted
• Payment contingent on validation

**8. Impact & Measurement (KPIs)**

DAO-controlled KPIs:
• Publication completed (yes/no)
• Number of media placements
• Links archived and shared publicly
Long-term value:
• Reusable editorial references
• External credibility
• Improved discoverability

**9. Risk & Mitigation**

Risks
• Limited immediate reach
• No viral effect
Mitigation
• Editorial content is durable
• Low budget exposure
• One-time expense
• No follow-up commitment without new vote

**10. Co-Funding & Leverage**

• No co-funding required
• No hidden dependencies
• No exclusivity or lock-in

**11. Contribution Equity & Compliance**

• Single contributor compensated (50,000 RBNT) for clearly scoped work
• No overlapping paid roles
• No RBNT used for speculation
• Vendor and contributor roles clearly separated
• Fully compliant with DAO conduct standards

**12. Community Involvement**

• Proposal discussed publicly prior to submission
• Deliverables shared openly with the community
• Outputs reusable by all DAO members
