**Context**
The current Snapshot voting period for DAO proposals is set to 3 days. This timeframe ensures quick decision-making, but it may also limit participation and discussion among leaders spread across different time zones.

**Question**
Should we extend the default Snapshot voting period beyond 3 days?

Instructions for Leaders

Please respond with Yes (extend) or No (keep at 3 days).

If Yes, also specify the number of days you believe is appropriate (e.g., 5, 7, or other).

**Purpose**
Your feedback will guide whether we adjust the default voting window to better balance efficiency and inclusive decision-making.
