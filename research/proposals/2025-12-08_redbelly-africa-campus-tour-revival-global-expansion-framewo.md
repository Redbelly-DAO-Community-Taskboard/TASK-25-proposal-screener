‎Category: Community Growth, Developer Adoption & Global Expansion
‎
‎This proposal requests DAO approval to launch the first pilot phase of the Redbelly Global Campus Tour Framework, a standardized, scalable system that any region can use to run high-quality developer and contributor onboarding events with unified messaging, KPIs, cost models, and educational materials.
‎
‎The first implementation is simply the controlled pilot, chosen for efficiency, speed, and clarity.
‎
‎Nigeria was selected as the pilot location for the Redbelly Global Campus Tour due to its status as Africa’s largest digital economy, its rapidly growing Web3 developer community, and its high concentration of active youth and university students. The country provides an ideal testing ground to validate engagement models, operational workflows, and onboarding strategies at scale before global rollout.
‎
‎The goal is global: the outputs of this pilot become the official Campus Tour Playbook that all Pod Leaders and Genesis Leaders worldwide can replicate in their own countries and regions with less friction and predictable budgeting.
‎
‎Countries in view: India, Singapore, United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates (UAE), Australia, Germany, South Korea, Brazil and many more!
