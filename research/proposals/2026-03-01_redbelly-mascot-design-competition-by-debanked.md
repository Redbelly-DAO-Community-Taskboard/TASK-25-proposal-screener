DAO PROPOSAL  
Redbelly Official Mascot Design Competition  by @debanked 
Open to the full Redbelly community and ecosystem

1.  Proposal Summary  
    This proposal launches a DAO-wide open competition to design an official Redbelly DAO mascot, with prizes awarded to winning entries and the DAO acquiring full usage rights to the selected character. The goal is to produce a high-quality, community-created, reusable brand asset suitable for marketing campaigns, educational content, live events, and ongoing social media presence.
    
2.  Problem & Rationale  
    Redbelly currently lacks a distinct and recognizable visual mascot that can humanize the brand, improve recall, and be reused consistently across all channels. Without such an asset, marketing materials rely on abstract or generic visuals that fail to build emotional connection with the audience.
    

A community competition directly addresses this gap while generating additional value:  
Encourages broad participation from designers and creators across the ecosystem  
Surfaces diverse creative ideas that internal teams may not produce alone  
Strengthens DAO culture by giving members a tangible stake in brand identity  
Ensures legitimacy through transparent community selection rather than top-down appointment

3.  Objectives & Strategic Fit  
    Objectives  
    Create an official DAO-owned mascot with full perpetual usage rights  
    Engage the community in a meaningful creative process  
    Generate reusable design assets for long-term brand use  
    Strengthen Redbelly's brand identity and visual consistency across channels  
    Strategic Fit  
    Community WG — drives engagement, incentivizes participation, and rewards contributors  
    Marketing WG — delivers a brand storytelling asset for campaigns and social media  
    DAO-wide goals — supports adoption and awareness through memorable visual identity

6.  Payment & Payout Structure  
    All payments are milestone-based — no upfront disbursements  
    RBNT finalist incentives released only after shortlist is verified and published (Phase 3)  
    USDT prizes released only after final asset delivery and IP rights transfer are confirmed (Phase 4)  
    RBNT community pool distributed post-competition based on verified participation  
    Full payout tables published publicly before the competition launches  
    Supervisor validates each milestone before any payout is approved
    
7.  Oversight & Accountability  
    An independent lead reviewer (Buffy) will be nominated from the DAO or Marketing Working Group prior to launch. This individual will not be eligible for any prizes and carries no financial interest in the outcome. The proposer will also be ineligible.
    

Oversight responsibilities include:  
Enforcing competition rules and eligibility criteria  
Verifying originality and intellectual property declarations for all submissions  
Approving the shortlist before public voting opens  
Validating final asset delivery and rights transfer before USDT disbursement  
Approving all milestone payouts before funds are released  
Publishing weekly progress updates in DAO channels  
Submitting a final public report upon competition close

8.  Impact & Measurement (KPIs)  
    Minimum 20 valid submissions received during the submission period  
    Community participation rate measured by unique wallet votes on finalists  
    Engagement tracked on all competition announcement posts across DAO channels  
    Complete mascot asset pack delivered in all required formats  
    Mascot adopted across DAO & community channels within 30 days of final delivery  
    Final report published within one week of competition close

10.  Co-Funding & Leverage  
    Community creators contribute their design work in exchange for prizes, meaning the DAO receives significant creative value at a fraction of market rate for commissioned artwork. The winning mascot becomes a long-term, reusable asset deployable across social media, events, documentation, education materials, and future brand campaigns — all at no additional cost.
    

Any future extensions such as animated versions, merchandise, or NFT variants would require separate DAO proposals and would not be covered by this budget.

11.  Contribution Equity  
    Prizes are distributed across multiple winners — no single individual receives a dominant share  
    RBNT rewards are spread across all verified finalists and community participants  
    The proposer and all oversight members are explicitly ineligible to win any prizes or finalist incentives  
    No single contributor holds multiple lead roles under this proposal without separate DAO approval
    
12.  Compliance & Ethical Standards  
    No financial advice is offered or implied by this proposal or competition process  
    The competition process is fully transparent — rules, scoring, and results published publicly  
    Only original, first-party work is accepted; all submissions must include an originality declaration  
    The DAO receives full, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free usage rights to the winning mascot upon prize payment  
    All finalist and final assets stored in DAO-accessible repositories for community use  
    Consistent with DAO code of conduct; no regulatory or reputational risks identified
    
13.  Community Involvement  
    Open submissions from all community members and ecosystem participants.  
    Community voting on the final shortlist — one verifiable wallet, one vote  
    Public discussion threads maintained throughout the competition period  
    All final assets shared openly for DAO-wide community use post-competition  
    This proposal itself is open for community feedback and co-authorship prior to formal submission
    

14.  Decision Requested
    

Approve 467,570 RBNT to run the DAO-governed Redbelly Community Mascot Design Competition — with milestone-based payouts, independent oversight, transparent community voting, and full perpetual IP rights acquisition for DAO-wide use.
