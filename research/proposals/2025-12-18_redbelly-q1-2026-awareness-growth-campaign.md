Overview

This proposal requests DAO approval to execute a 4-week awareness and growth campaign for Redbelly DAO in Q1 2026.
The campaign is designed to expand Redbelly’s visibility, educate users on DAO governance and RBNT utility, and increase long-term community participation while producing reusable marketing assets for the DAO.

All activities will be milestone-based, transparent, and independently reviewed to ensure accountability and impact.

Objectives

The campaign focuses on four core goals:

Increase Redbelly awareness across Web3 audiences

Educate users on DAO participation, governance, and RBNT utility

Grow active and engaged community members

Create reusable content and marketing assets for future DAO use

Scope of Work & Deliverables

The campaign will deliver:

10+ branded visual assets (educational and promotional)

3 short explainer videos (Redbelly DAO, governance, RBNT utility)

2 recorded X/Twitter Spaces focused on awareness and education

Community engagement activities (quizzes, challenges, threads)

A comprehensive analytics and performance report at completion

All content will remain available for reuse by the DAO after the campaign.

Budget & DAO Compliance

Total Request:

4,500 USDT (external marketing costs)

200,000 RBNT (internal incentives)

The budget:

Remains below 25% of the Marketing Working Group quarterly allocation

Complies with the DAO-wide quarterly limits

Respects the 90% reserve policy

Includes no token purchases, personal expenses, or unverifiable costs

USDT Usage Includes:

Research & analytics tools

Content and video production

Community activation activities

Distribution and outreach

Small DAO-approved contingency reserve

RBNT Allocation:

Contributor incentives (research, content, video)

Community participation rewards

Validator and reviewer incentives

Payment & Transparency

All payouts are milestone-based

Limited upfront payments only where necessary

The Campaign Lead receives no USDT and 30,000 RBNT only

No contributor receives more than 35% of total compensation

All payouts follow Web3 and NGO cost-efficiency standards

Timeline & Execution

Week 1: Preparation – branding, templates, analytics setup

Week 2: Awareness – content rollout and collaborations

Week 3: Engagement – X Spaces, optimization, metrics review

Week 4: Activation – quizzes, final Space, reporting & verification

Oversight & Accountability

Independent oversight by a non-contributing Marketing WG reviewer

Weekly progress updates shared with the DAO

Final report published at campaign completion

Oversight has authority to delay or withhold payouts if milestones are not met

Impact Measurement (KPIs)

The campaign targets:

500,000+ total impressions

1,000+ new participants

30% increase in DAO engagement

Risks & Mitigation

Potential risks include low engagement, delays, or budget inefficiencies.
Mitigation strategies include pre-scoped assets, weekly reviews, influencer collaboration, and strict budget controls.

Decision Requested

Approve 4,500 USDT and 200,000 RBNT to execute the Redbelly Q1 2026 Awareness & Growth Campaign with milestone-based payouts and independent oversight.
