Summary  
Following the successful completion of the DAO's first media campaign delivered by FINPR, this proposal seeks approval for a three-month continuation program focused on increasing Redbelly DAO and ecosystem awareness through targeted PR placements, influencer marketing, and social media management.  
The objective is to build upon the momentum already generated, maintain consistent market visibility, and create sustained awareness around Redbelly's ecosystem, institutional positioning, and community initiatives.

Problem & Rationale  
The DAO's initial media campaign successfully established visibility across crypto media outlets and demonstrated FINPR's ability to deliver agreed promotional activities.  
Marketing impact compounds over time. One-off campaigns often generate temporary attention, while consistent visibility creates stronger brand recognition, audience growth, and ecosystem awareness.  
Redbelly's ecosystem continues to evolve through institutional adoption initiatives, RWA infrastructure development, strategic partnerships, and community governance growth. Maintaining ongoing media exposure aligns with the DAO's objective of supporting awareness and ecosystem expansion.  
The proposal therefore seeks to continue the relationship with FINPR through a structured three-month campaign.
