DAO PROPOSAL: Redbelly “X Tipping Campaign” – Quarter 1 Pilot (1 Quarter Only)
A community-driven incentive program to amplify high-quality Redbelly content across X.
Submitted by: @Buffy_NFTs
Working Group: Community & Marketing
Date: 07/12/2025
Funding Request: USDT + RBNT (details in Section 6)
1. Proposal Summary (1–2 Sentences)
This proposal requests DAO approval to launch a one quarter pilot “X Tipping Campaign,” administered by seven paid DAO elected Leaders, where the community votes on the best content created using the following hashtags - #RedbellyDAO #RedbellyNetwork #RBNT. RBNT is distributed as tips to reward high-quality educational, technical, and awareness content. The program aims to increase Redbelly’s visibility, improve brand consistency, and grow an active social ecosystem. Anybody on x can turn on tipping and link a wallet in order to receive “tips”.
2. Problem & Rationale
Problem
Redbelly currently lacks high-quality, decentralized community-driven content on X. Existing activity is fragmented, engagement is inconsistent, and organic reach is low.
Rationale
This program directly addresses:
Low visibility
Lack of structured grassroots marketing
Need for community-led educational content
Difficulty attracting new audiences
Why a Tipping Campaign Works
Incentivises quality, not quantity
Anyone on X or LinkedIn (posting via X) can participate — no KYC required to receive the tip, only to move RBNT
Community voting ensures decentralisation and fairness
Encourages creativity, competitiveness, and education-first messaging
Uses RBNT for its intended purpose: ecosystem growth
3. Objectives & Strategic Fit

Strategic Fit
Aligns with:

4. Scope of Work & Timeline
Scope
Launch a 12-week tipping incentive program
Discord Elections in the DAO channel (can be self-nominated) to appoint 7 active DAO Leaders, as campaign administrators who receive a weekly stipend for the duration of the campaign of $150USDT each. Each Leader is allocated a day of the week where they perform the following tasks. Max of four hours expected to complete the following:
Review daily relevant posts using the following #: #RedbellyDAO #RedbellyNetwork #RBNT/$RBNT
Run one/multiple polls to determine best 5 posts that day – try to get a variety of posts, Youtubes, Medium Articles, threads, AMA’s etc, individual polls for each different type of post eg poll with 3 best basic posts, poll with 3 best youtubes, poll with 3 best Medium articles etc.…
All polls for this campaign conducted within Discord DAO channels to prevent botting
Once 5 winners for the day determined (preferably minimum of one from each category), wallet copied from their tips account, submitted with a link to the post to a Google Form.
Daily 5 winning posts also retweeted by DAO account once selected.
Tips given out once per week, and double checked to ensure no posts doubled up – if duplicated, only valid for one tip.
Each Leader has a max of 20,000 RBNT to Tip per day (140,000 RBNT per week). See below for categories to split between. If no entries for a particular category for that day, that amount can be carried forward and any left overs pooled and exceptional posts can receive “extra” at discretion of the DAO.
Track reach, engagement, participation, and onboarding metrics via links to the posts
Deliver end-of-quarter report
