Constitutional Amendment

5. Strategy & Performance Officer
Strategic planning

These positions replace the existing generic Leadership positions and provide clearly defined operational responsibilities.

The replacement of position titles is intended to improve organisational clarity and accountability only and does not alter the governance authority of the High Council or the constitutional powers of the DAO community.

The names and responsibilities of Operational Leadership portfolios may only be amended by an approved constitutional amendment unless the Constitution expressly provides otherwise.

Executive Summary
This proposal clarifies the governance structure of the Redbelly Community DAO by distinguishing between elected governance positions and appointed operational leadership positions.

The High Council will continue to be elected by the community and retain governance authority.
Operational Leadership positions will instead be appointed through a transparent, merit-based process, recognising that these positions primarily perform operational and administrative functions requiring appropriate skills, experience and availability.

This proposal preserves democratic governance while strengthening the operational capability, accountability and continuity of the DAO.

Background
The Constitution currently provides for elected High Council and Leadership positions.

Experience has demonstrated that Leadership positions perform operational rather than governance functions. These roles often require specialised skills and ongoing commitment that may not always be reflected through popularity-based elections.

This proposal aligns the governance framework with the original intent of the Constitution by ensuring governance remains democratic while operational responsibilities are undertaken by suitably qualified contributors.

Problem & Rationale
Operational Leadership positions are responsible for functions such as:
Treasury administration
Governance administration
Proposal management
Technical coordination
Ecosystem liaison
Community engagement
Strategic planning
These responsibilities are better suited to transparent merit-based appointments than popularity-based elections.
The community should continue electing those responsible for governance while enabling operational roles to be filled according to competence and capability.

Objectives

Proposed Constitutional Amendment
Governance Structure
High Council

The High Council shall continue to be elected by the DAO community.

The High Council remains responsible for:
Governance oversight
Constitutional compliance
Treasury oversight
Strategic governance
Appointment oversight
Accountability to the community

This proposal does not alter the number of High Council positions established elsewhere in the Constitution.

Operational Leadership
The following positions shall become appointed Operational Leadership positions:
Treasurer
Governance & Proposals Officer
Technical & Ecosystem Liaison
Community & Communications Officer
Strategy & Performance Officer

These positions perform administrative and operational functions and do not independently exercise governance authority unless expressly authorised by the Constitution or an approved DAO proposal.
 
Appointments should seek to match each Operational Leadership portfolio with individuals possessing relevant qualifications, experience or demonstrated capability appropriate to the responsibilities of that portfolio.

Appointment Process
Operational Leadership appointments shall be made by the High Council through majority resolution following an open merit-based recruitment process. Appointment decisions, including the reasons for selection, shall be published to the community. Any community member may challenge an appointment through the Governance Appeals & Review Policy where they believe the appointment process has not been conducted in accordance with the Constitution or approved Governance Policies.
Where an elected position remains vacant, or where insufficient suitably qualified candidates are available, the High Council may appoint a suitably qualified individual to serve until the next scheduled election, subject to transparency requirements.

Appointments should consider:
Relevant experience
Demonstrated capability
Governance participation
Availability
Integrity
Communication skills
Absence of material conflicts of interest

Recruitment Process
Appointments should normally include:
Public expression of interest
Published selection criteria
Opportunity for self-nomination
Transparent appointment decision
Publication of appointment reasons

Terms of Appointment
Operational Leadership appointments should normally:
Be for a six (6) month term with one extension.
Remain eligible for reappointment.
Be reviewed annually.
Remain accountable to the High Council.

Responsibilities
Operational Leadership shall:
Fulfil the responsibilities of their assigned portfolio.
Publish periodic activity reports.
Maintain governance documentation.
Support community transparency.
Cooperate with other leadership portfolios.
Act in accordance with the Constitution, Governance Policies and Code of Conduct.
Inactivity
An Operational Leadership member may be considered inactive where they have failed to meaningfully participate in the responsibilities of their appointed role for a continuous period of thirty (30) days without reasonable explanation or prior notification to the High Council.
Meaningful participation may include:
Responding to governance matters.
Undertaking the responsibilities of the position.
Attending scheduled leadership meetings where reasonably practicable.
Providing required reports or updates.
The High Council may extend this period where reasonable circumstances exist, including illness, family responsibilities, employment commitments or other exceptional circumstances.
Where a member is determined inactive under this clause, the High Council may declare the position vacant by majority resolution.

Performance Review
An Operational Leadership appointment may be terminated for unsatisfactory performance or serious misconduct, including material breach of the Code of Conduct, where the High Council has determined, by majority resolution, that the member has persistently failed to fulfil the responsibilities of their appointed role despite having been provided with reasonable opportunity to address identified concerns.

Removal Process
Prior to removal, the Operational Leadership member should:
Be informed of the concerns.
Be provided an opportunity to respond.
Be given reasonable opportunity to address the concerns where appropriate.
Following consideration of all relevant information, the High Council may remove the appointment by majority resolution.
The decision and reasons for removal should be documented and made available to the community, except where confidentiality is required by law or to protect personal privacy.

Appeals
Any Operational Leadership member removed under this provision may request a review under the Governance Appeals & Review Policy.

Vacancies
Temporary Appointment of Foundation Personnel
Where:
no suitably qualified community applicant is available following a documented recruitment process; or
an Operational Leadership position remains vacant following reasonable efforts to appoint a community member; and
the continued vacancy presents a material operational risk to the DAO,
the High Council may request that the Redbelly Foundation nominate a suitably qualified employee or contractor to temporarily perform the responsibilities of the vacant Operational Leadership position.
Any such appointment shall:
be temporary only;
ineligible for filling the Treasurer role specifically due to conflict of interest
be approved by the High Council by majority resolution;
not exceed twelve (12) months without further DAO approval;
remain subject to the Constitution, Governance Policies and Code of Conduct;
be publicly announced together with the reasons for the appointment.
Where an Operational Leadership position becomes vacant, the High Council may appoint an interim replacement by majority resolution until the position is filled through the normal appointment process.
This proposal does not alter constitutional provisions relating to elected High Council vacancies.
Governance Principles
Operational Leadership exists to support the effective administration of the DAO.
Appointments should be based primarily upon competence, experience, integrity and the ability to fulfil the responsibilities of the role rather than popularity.
