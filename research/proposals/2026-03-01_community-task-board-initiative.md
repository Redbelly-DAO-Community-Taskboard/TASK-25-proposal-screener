## What is this?

The Redbelly DAO has skilled, motivated community members with nowhere structured to direct their energy. This proposal creates a **Community Task Board**, a system where contributors can pick up real tasks, do real work, and earn RBNT rewards for it. Documentation, research, content, developer tools, ecosystem building, and more.

The task board runs on the existing DAO website infrastructure. No new platforms. No bloat. Just a structured way to get things done.

---

## Why now?

- The DAO treasury has resources but no clear pathway for community contributors beyond large one-off proposals
- Developers building DAO infrastructure have no formal audit or peer review loop
- Documentation and tutorials for builders are lacking, slowing ecosystem growth
- Project Acacia Q1 2026 report drops soon, a time-sensitive research opportunity we should not miss
- Other L1s are building comprehensive developer ecosystems. We should too.

---

## How it works

**6 task categories:**

- **A. Documentation & Tutorials:** guides, video tutorials, developer onboarding content
- **B. Content & Community:** infographics, case studies, Twitter threads, Scout role for ecosystem content
- **C. Research & Analysis:** competitive intelligence, RWA market research, Project Acacia deep-dive
- **D. Developer Work:** smart contract templates, CI/CD tooling, peer review and audit of DAO-built infrastructure
- **E. Ecosystem Building:** compliance templates, partnership research, service provider directories
- **F. Strategic Coordination:** monthly education and technical working meetings

**Anyone can propose tasks.** A Google Form will be set up in Week 1 for open nominations from community members and core team alike.

**All payments in RBNT,** except video production tasks which include a 30% USDT component to cover software costs.

---

## Budget

Two separate budgets. No conversion between them.

| Budget | Quarterly Cap | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RBNT | $4,500 USD equivalent | All tasks except video. Fixed in RBNT at proposal approval using 7-day moving average, reset each quarter. |
| USDT | $500 | Video task production costs only (30% component) |
| **Total** | **$5,000** | Hard quarterly cap. Auto-pause if either budget is exhausted. |

---

## Rollout

**Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Pilot with 10-12 tasks, 3-5 reviewers, quality standards established.
**Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Scale to 20-25 tasks/month, bug bounty program launches, reputation tiers introduced.
**Phase 3 (Months 7-12):** Stable operations, contributor pipeline feeding reviewer roles, outputs integrated into official docs.

Quarterly DAO votes determine continuation at each phase.

---

## Accountability

- Every deliverable reviewed before payment
- Weekly updates in a dedicated Discord channel
- Monthly report across Discord, X, and Telegram
- Quarterly review with DAO vote
- Hard kill switch conditions if quality drops or budget is hit
