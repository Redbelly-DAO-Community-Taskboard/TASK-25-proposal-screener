Overview

This proposal introduces an 8-week, research-driven educational initiative focused on improving transparency, technical clarity, and ecosystem understanding of Redbelly Network.
The output includes independent research reports and high-quality YouTube explainer videos that the DAO can reuse for onboarding, marketing, partnerships, and documentation.

The initiative runs for 8 weeks, with community checkpoints and DAO input at major
milestones.

problems
Redbelly promotes strong claims (high throughput, deterministic finality, BFT
performance), but there is very limited independent research verifying or explaining
these claims in detail.
This makes it difficult for:
developers,
validators,
community
institutions, and
potential partners
to fully understand Redbelly’s architecture and advantages.
Unclear Tokenomics & Economic Model.
Currently the token utility, distribution, and long-term incentive structure is not widely understood.
Key questions remain researched but not clearly understood from non technical points
How does RBNT secure the network?
Is inflation planned or fixed?
What happens to validator rewards over time?
How sustainable is the current staking model?
