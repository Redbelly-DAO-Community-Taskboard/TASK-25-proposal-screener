**Summary **

This proposal requests funding of **$350 USDT per month** for the continued development, hosting, maintenance, and operation of the official Redbelly DAO Discord Bot, along with ongoing Discord community management and engagement activities.

The bot is designed to become a central hub for DAO members by simplifying governance, improving communication, and supporting contributor coordination. It also provides a foundation for future DAO features such as task management, contributor notifications, and community engagement.


** Problem & Rationale**

As the Redbelly DAO grows, community members use several different platforms to access governance updates, participate in discussions, and coordinate work. This can make it difficult to stay informed and engaged.

A dedicated Discord bot provides a single place where members can access governance information, receive important notifications, and, in the future, manage contributor tasks without leaving Discord.

Combining bot development with community management ensures that both the technical platform and the community continue to grow together.



**Objectives**

The objectives of this proposal are to:

* Improve communication within the DAO.
* Increase participation in governance.
* Provide automated notifications for DAO activities.
* Build a task management system for contributors.
* Support onboarding and engagement of community members.
* Create a more active and organized Discord community.



** Current Features**

The bot already includes:

* Interactive DAO dashboard
* Live Snapshot proposal integration
* Active and past proposal browsing
* Proposal details and voting information
* Wallet linking
* Member profiles
* Participation leaderboard
* Automatic notifications for new proposals
* Voting reminder notifications



** Planned Features**

During this funding period, development will focus on expanding the bot with:

**Task Management**

* Members can view available DAO tasks.
* Members can accept tasks.
* Members can submit completed work.
* Administrators can approve or reject submitted tasks.
* Automatic notifications when new tasks become available.
* Status updates for accepted and completed tasks.

** Community Management**

* Discord moderation and support.
* New member onboarding.
* Community announcements.
* Community games and engagement events.
* General server management.
* Regular improvements based on community feedback.



 **Deliverables**

Each month includes:

* 24/7 bot hosting.
* Ongoing maintenance and bug fixes.
* New feature development.
* Discord community management.
* Community engagement activities.
* Technical support.
* Performance monitoring.
* Regular updates and improvements.
