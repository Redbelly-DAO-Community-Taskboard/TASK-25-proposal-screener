We’re opening voting for our next leadership cohort. 

**There are 12 nominated candidates and 8 positions available for a 6-month term. **

Top 3 ranked candidates will form the High Council, next 5 ranked candidates will take on Leadership roles.

This is a single ranked vote covering all 8 positions. Please rank all candidates in your order of preference, from strongest to weakest, to ensure the results reflect overall community sentiment. 

All nominated candidates have shared their pitches in Discord → #campaign-forum.  We encourage you to read through these before voting so you can make an informed decision.  

Voting will use shielded voting. All choices are encrypted and results will only be visible once voting has closed, to encourage fair and unbiased participation. 

Thank you for taking the time to participate and help shape the direction of the DAO.
