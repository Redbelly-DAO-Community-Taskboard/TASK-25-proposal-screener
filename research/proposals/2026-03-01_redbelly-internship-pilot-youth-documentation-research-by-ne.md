**Redbelly Internship Pilot – Youth Documentation & Research  
Initiative (Philippines)**  
Submitted By  
**Discord: nekoyamaneki**  
  
Working Group: Community (Secondary: Research / Marketing)  
Duration: 3 Months

1.  **Proposal Summary**  
    This proposal introduces a 3‑month internship-style program for three Filipino IT/CS  
    students who will document RBNT activity into a structured archive and compile RWA  
    competitor-related updates. The initiative supports students in fulfilling internship  
    requirements while providing Redbelly DAO with a persistent documentation asset useful  
    for onboarding, historical context, and research reference.
    
2.  **Problem & Rationale**  
    Redbelly currently lacks a consolidated archive of announcements, published materials, and  
    ecosystem updates. Information is distributed across multiple platforms, making  
    onboarding new contributors inefficient and time-consuming.  
    At the same time, Filipino students often lack access to relevant, technology-aligned  
    internships. By combining structured documentation work with supervised milestone  
    validation, this initiative provides real internship experience while generating long‑term  
    value for the DAO.
    
3.  **Objectives & Strategic Fit**  
    Objectives  
     Compile a structured record of RBNT public activity  
     Track relevant competitor mentions and updates  
     Provide students with verifiable internship-aligned experience  
     Produce documentation that remains useful after project completion  
    Strategic Fit  
     Community onboarding  
     Ecosystem education  
     Contributor development  
     Research support  
    This project establishes a repeatable documentation pipeline model that can expand if  
    successful.
    
4.  **Scope of Work & Deliverables**  
    Month 1  
     Documentation framework setup  
     Begin documentation from proposal approval date forward  
     Initial competitor tracking log initiated  
     Supervisor + Independent Reviewer validation  
    Month 2  
     Ongoing RBNT activity documented  
     Notable competitor updates summarized  
     Formatting refinement and structure improvement  
     Supervisor + Independent Reviewer validation  
    Month 3  
     Final consolidated document prepared  
     Summary notes for onboarding usability  
     Completion confirmation for all students  
     Supervisor + Independent Reviewer validation
    
5.  **Deliverable Structure & Success Criteria**  
    Deliverable Format  
     Date or time period  
     Source (Redbelly Network official channels)  
     Direct link or reference  
    Scope Clarification  
    Documentation begins from proposal approval date forward. Historical backfill is not  
    included in this phase. The priority is establishing a reliable documentation framework and  
    capturing live RBNT activity consistently.  
    Competitor tracking will be limited to directly comparable RWA or infrastructure-adjacent  
    projects and include only notable public updates or mentions. The intent is contextual  
    awareness rather than exhaustive monitoring.  
    Success Metrics & Quality Standards  
     Clearly structured and consistently formatted documentation file  
     Defined coverage for the agreed documentation window  
     Monthly milestone reviews confirming accuracy, formatting consistency, and  
    completeness  
    Milestone approval requires validation by both the Supervisor and Independent Reviewer.
    
6.  **Budget & Payment Structure**  
    Total Requested: 1,650 USDT (550 USDT per month × 3 months)  
     Intern Pool: 450 USDT/month (150 each)  
     Supervisor: 100 USDT/month  
     Paid only after verified deliverables  
    Payment Terms  
     Paid monthly after review and acceptance  
     No upfront disbursements  
     DAO carries zero upfront treasury risk  
     Supervisor distributes intern allocations internally  
     Confirmation of intern payment distribution will be transparently reported in milestone  
    updates
    
7.  **Oversight & Accountability**  
    Supervisor: nekoyamaneki  
    Independent Reviewer: Buffy  
     Validates monthly deliverables before payment approval  
     Confirms documentation accuracy and formatting standards  
     Ensures milestone compliance  
     Remains independent from execution and compensation  
    Payments released only after both Supervisor and Reviewer confirmation.
    
8.  **Impact & Measurement (KPIs)**  
     Structured archive established  
     Ongoing documentation maintained  
     Competitor updates tracked  
     Students complete milestones  
     Final documentation reusable as reference  
    Expanded Impact Metrics  
     Repository access or download count (if measurable through DAO‑controlled storage)
    
9.  **Risks & Mitigation**  
     Intern delays — payments tied to completion  
     Inaccuracies — dual review validation  
     Formatting issues — structured standards enforced  
     Limited usefulness — readability requirement before approval
    
10.  **Co‑ Funding & Leverage**  
    No external co‑funding requested.  
     Reusable documentation asset  
     Reduced onboarding friction  
     Contributor pipeline development  
     Educational alignment with DAO mission
    
11.  **Contribution Equity**  
     Fair distribution across interns  
     Modest supervisor allocation  
     No payout concentration  
     Clear separation of roles
    
12.  **Compliance & Ethical Standards**  
    This proposal involves documentation of publicly available information only and does not  
    involve financial custody, token purchases, regulatory exposure, or representation on behalf  
    of the DAO. All activities align with DAO ethical standards.
    
13.  **Community Visibility**  
     Monthly progress updates shared in DAO channels  
     Final documentation delivered to official repository  
     Supports onboarding and historical reference
    
14.  **Post‑ Pilot Ownership & Continuity**  
    Upon completion, the archive becomes a DAO‑owned resource. Students and supervisor are  
    responsible only for agreed deliverables during the pilot period. After Month 3, ownership  
    and expansion fall under DAO governance.  
    This is a one‑time archival pilot and does not create an obligation for continued funding or  
    maintenance.  
    Future contributors or working groups may submit new proposals to extend or expand the  
    archive if the DAO determines value.
    
15.  **Implementation Clarifications**  
    Final Document Storage  
    The final file will be delivered to the Independent Reviewer for submission to an official  
    DAO repository.  
    This pilot focuses strictly on manual structured documentation and does not include  
    infrastructure or automation development.  
    Success Evaluation  
     Archive completion  
     Accuracy and organization  
     Validated milestone approvals
