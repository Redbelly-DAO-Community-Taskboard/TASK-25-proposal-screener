1-Month Pilot Version (January Pilot)
Prepared by: Ape
1. Pilot Purpose
This 1-month pilot tests whether a streamlined Education & Community program can consistently
deliver high-quality content, improve onboarding, and grow Redbelly’s visibility across TikTok, X,
Instagram and YouTube Shorts before committing to a full-quarter program.
2. Pilot Budget (1-Month Only)
USDT Budget — 1-Month Pilot (Reduced Version) Total: 900 USDT
• 300 USDT — Boosted ads
• 250 USDT — Creative/editing tools
• 200 USDT — X Tips incentives
• 150 USDT — Contingency buffer
RBNT Budget — 1-Month Pilot Total: 50,000 RBNT
• 18,000 RBNT — Graphics
• 15,000 RBNT — Videos
• 10,000 RBNT — Written explainers
• 7,000 RBNT — Quizzes & incentives
3. Deliverables
Weekly:
• 1 educational video
• 1–2 educational images
• 1 written explainer post
• Starter Pack weekly update
• Weekly analytics summary
Monthly:
• 4 videos, 4–8 images, 4 explainers, full monthly dashboard
4. Milestone-Based Payments
Zero-upfront model; contributors paid only upon:
• Weekly deliverable completion + proof links
• Starter Pack update
• Weekly analytics
• Monthly dashboard + reviewer approval
5. Strategic Fit
Supports Marketing, Community and Research Pods by enhancing education, onboarding and
multi-platform growth.
6. Oversight & Governance
• Weekly validation of all deliverables ensures consistent quality, accountability, and timely
feedback loops.
• Reviewer + Backup Reviewer structure avoids concentration of power and prevents single-point
control or bottlenecks.
• Source files stored for transparency, plus a public payout sheet.
7. KPIs
• 60k+ monthly impressions
• 15%+ engagement growth
• 20%+ watch-through rate
• 50+ Starter Pack users
8. Risk & Mitigation
Multi-platform strategy, targeted boosts, reviewer validation and capped deliverables prevent
burnout.
9. Co-Funding
Ape contributes daily organic content at no cost, increasing ROI.
10. Contribution Equity
No contributor receives over 25% of rewards.
11. Compliance
All content is educational, original/licensed, and non-financial advice.
