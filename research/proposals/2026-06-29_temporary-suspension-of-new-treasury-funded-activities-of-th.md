# Summary
This proposal seeks to temporarily suspend the operational activities of the Redbelly Community DAO until the broader Redbelly ecosystem reaches a level of maturity where the DAO can efficiently achieve its stated objectives.
The proposal is intended to preserve treasury resources, reduce operational overhead, and allow the DAO to resume when there is a stronger foundation for meaningful community-led initiatives.
No existing approved obligations would be affected.


# Problem & Rationale
The Constitution states that the Community DAO exists to support the growth, adoption and decentralisation of the Redbelly Community DAO.
The proposer believes that the ecosystem is still in an early stage where maintaining continuous treasury-funded activity may not currently provide the highest return for the community.
A temporary suspension would allow the DAO to preserve financial resources and avoid unnecessary expenditure until conditions better support effective community governance and measurable outcomes.
This proposal is intended as a treasury preservation measure rather than a permanent closure.


# Objectives
Preserve DAO treasury assets.
Reduce unnecessary operational expenditure.
Complete all currently approved commitments.
Pause funding of new initiatives during the suspension.
Resume DAO operations through a future governance vote when ecosystem readiness improves.
