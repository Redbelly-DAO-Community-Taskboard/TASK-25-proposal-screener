# LIVE - Redbelly Community DAO Constitution - V1.2

## Preamble

The Redbelly Community DAO exists to steward the growth, adoption, and decentralisation of the Redbelly Community DAO (not Redbelly Network itself). This Constitution defines how the DAO operates, allocates resources, and empowers contributors. While initially top-down for efficiency and integrity, the DAO will progressively decentralise as its contributor base and processes mature.

---

## 1. Treasury Overview

### Assets:

- Token Treasury: 10,000,000 RBNT — for incentives, rewards, grants

- Stablecoin Reserve: 100,000 USDT annually — for operational costs and contributor payments

- Revenue Source: 2.5% of network fees streamed to the DAO treasury monthly

---

## 2. Governance Structure

### 2.1 Governance Phases

Each phase will have target timelines, reviewed quarterly and published publicly by the High Council. If dependencies outside the DAO’s control delay progress, revised timelines will be shared to prevent indefinite stalling.

### 2.2 Treasury Management

- The DAO treasury is held in a secure multisig wallet (e.g. Gnosis Safe) with public signers.

- All use of treasury funds must follow the defined proposal process and receive approval.

- Network fees (2.5%) are automatically streamed into the treasury monthly via smart contract.

### 2.3 Voting Power

- Phase 0 & 1: Voting power is proportional to RBNT held or delegated (weighted voting)

- Genesis Leaders and Pod Leads may vote, but Pod Leads may not cast more than one vote per proposal

- Conflict of Interest: Individuals must recuse from voting on their own proposals

---

## 3. Working Groups (Pods)

Each Pod has a Lead and contributors. Leads are responsible for reporting, coordination, and output delivery.

### 🟠 Marketing Pod

Mission: Drive awareness, growth, and visibility for the Redbelly ecosystem.

### 🛠️ Builder/Develop Pod

Mission: Empower technical contributors and strengthen the developer ecosystem.

### 🧠 Researcher Pod

Mission: Generate insights to guide DAO strategy and ecosystem awareness.

### 🌱 Community Pod

Mission: Build an inclusive and informed community of contributors and token holders.

### 🤝 Partnerships Pod

Mission: Expand the DAO’s reach through meaningful partnerships and integrations.

> Substructures like “Scouts” or “Echo Rangers” are paused for now to maintain simplicity.

Each pod is responsible for quarterly reporting and transparent use of allocated funds.

---

## 4. Governance Process/Proposal Lifecycle

### 4.1 Process Overview

1. Submit proposal via Notion form

1. Guild Review by relevant Pod

1. Cross-Pod Feedback via Discord (3-day window)

1. High Council Review (budget & role clarity)

1. Vote via Snapshot (for projects that have more than >5k RBNT/USDT) or Discord (for holders with <5k RBNT)

1. Contributor Onboarded via Dework (if applicable)

1. Execution phase begins

1. Final Review by Guild + Council

1. Payment + Archive (with public record)

### 4.2 Open Innovation Track

- For submissions outside pod mandates

- Pilot limit: 100,000 RBNT or 5,000 USDT

- Still follows full review and vote process

---

## 5. Conflict of Interest Policy

- Pod Leads, Council Members, and Reviewers must publicly disclose relevant relationships

- Recusal is required for any vote on self-submitted proposals

- Limit: One paid role per individual per quarter

- Anonymous reviewers (if used) must submit written rationale; logs must be audit-friendly

---

## 6. Treasury Management

### 6.1 Transparency and Accountability

- Monthly treasury and revenue dashboards published to the community

- Quarterly reporting required by each working group

- Public access to all proposals, vote results, and treasury activity

### 6.2 Budgeting

- Annual cap: 100,000 USDT

- RBNT: 10M total available for DAO incentives

- Quarterly splits to Pods to be published at start of each quarter

- 10% minimum allocation reserved for Open Innovation

### 6.2 Transparency

- Monthly treasury dashboards (spend + income)

- Quarterly Pod reporting on spend, outcomes, and KPIs

---

## 7. Contributor Expectations

- RBNT and/or USDT may be awarded through:

  - Contributor roles and grants

  - Community quests and bounties

  - Workstream-specific reward programs

- Tasks and grants must define:

  - Clear KPIs

  - Timeline and milestones

  - Reward structure (RBNT/USDT)

- Missed deadlines without comms may result in reassignment and frozen payment

Where appropriate, contributors may also be rewarded in tokens directly related to supported projects or initiatives, to better align incentives with ecosystem value creation.

---

## 8. Community Involvement

- Genesis Leaders will hold early voting power, but full DAO access will open progressively

- Founding Principles will be reviewed and proposed by the Community Pod in collaboration with other Genesis Leaders

- All approved contributors will be acknowledged via:

  - Galxe badge

DAO participation is community-based and voluntary; responsibilities equivalent to formal employment within the Redbelly core team remain outside the DAO’s scope. This separation ensures fairness between community contributors and formal employees

  - DAO report mentions

  - Public archive of completed work

---

## 9. Amendments

Changes to this Constitution must follow:

- 7-day proposal notice

- 5-day voting period with all DAO members (as well as High Council, Pod Leaders, Genesis Leaders as part of this)

- Majority approval via token-weighted vote (or Genesis vote in Phase 0)

- Version control and public publishing required for each update

## 10. Founding Principles

- Transparency and Auditability

All proposals, votes, budgets, and outputs should be publicly visible, with rationale and documentation wherever possible.

- Fair Access, with Structured Onboarding

Participation should be open, but gated by contribution and reputation, not popularity or influence.

- Merit-Based Funding and Roles

Funding is earned through clear deliverables and value — not connections or titles. No role is permanent, and all are accountable.

- Progressive Decentralisation

DAO governance will start with strong leadership but shift toward wider community control as capacity and clarity grow.

- Constructive Collaboration over Silos

Pods should collaborate across domains and surface disagreements early — no single pod should dominate DAO direction.

- Alignment with Redbelly’s Infrastructure Vision

DAO activity must reinforce Redbelly’s broader ecosystem goals: scalable, secure, and institution-grade blockchain infrastructure.

---

## Appendix: Definitions

- High Council: Bootstrap group responsible for interim budget, strategy, and mediation

- Pod Lead: Appointed or elected operator of a working group with execution responsibility

- Genesis Leader: Early DAO participant with temporary elevated voting privileges

- Contributor: Anyone completing tasks for the DAO in return for RBNT or USDT
